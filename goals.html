The demo counts are local to this page view. Real counts are aggregated by your backend and read in the console’s Goals page.

How it works

  • Page view goals match a page key exactly, after grouping. Click goals match an action name on any page. Event goals match a custom event name.
  • Up to 20 goals per website. Reports show conversions, visitors, rate and trend. Aggregate counts only.
  • Adding and deleting needs a website-owner or admin key; analyst keys read only.
