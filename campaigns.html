Payload

Only the landing page view carries the labels. Labels are lowercased and cleaned in the browser, then again by the backend.

https://example.com/pricing?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=spring-sale

{
  "page": "/pricing",
  "campaign": { "source": "newsletter", "medium": "email", "campaign": "spring-sale" }
}

How it works

  • Three labels only: utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign. utm_term, utm_content, gclid and fbclid are dropped.
  • Cleaning: lowercased, emails become [email], long digit runs [number], token-like words [token], only letters, digits, spaces and _ - . + : / kept, cut to 64 characters.
  • Attribution is by landing page only. No cross-visit attribution, because no visitor identifiers are kept.
  • Referrers are reported separately; the two are not merged.
