How it works

  • An action is a click, Enter or Space on a button, a[href], button-like input, or an element with role button, link, menuitem or tab.
  • The name comes from, in order: data-vizoalica-action, aria-label, visible text (or value), title, image alt, then “Unlabeled button/link/control”.
  • Names are cleaned in browser and backend: emails → [email], 6+ digit runs → [number], token-like words → [token], cut at 80 characters. Short numbers like 8841 are not detected: name such controls explicitly.
  • A repeated click on the same control within 500 ms counts once; at most 100 actions a minute per page load.

Never recorded: typed text, field values, clicks on plain text, images or empty space, anything in cross-site iframes, link queries and fragments, mailto:/tel:, positions, element identifiers. There is no session replay or heatmap.
